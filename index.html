Percentages are each venue’s implied probability of the team winning. Cells are shaded in each team’s primary color. Kalshi and Polymarket use the midpoint of the live best bid and ask (or the last trade if there is no two-sided book); FanDuel is converted from its moneyline with the vig removed. A FanDuel box flashes yellow when its line moves. Kalshi and Polymarket refresh about every 20 seconds, and FanDuel (via The Odds API) every few minutes to save API credits. Green outline = lowest probability for that team across venues. Spread = gap between the highest and lowest venue, flagged at 4+ points. A venue shows “—” if it has no matching market.
